Nigeria's education sector received sustained support through the Tertiary Education Trust Fund, which the Centre for Public Accountability described as vital to preventing severe infrastructural decline in public institutions [8]. Between 2011 and 2024, TETFund disbursed over ₦1.8 trillion to public tertiary institutions, with universities receiving more than ₦918 billion, polytechnics over ₦461 billion, and colleges of education exceeding ₦458 billion [8]. These interventions resulted in more than 152,000 infrastructural projects nationwide, including lecture theatres, laboratories, libraries, hostels, and ICT centres [8]. The organisation noted that institutions previously suffering from severe infrastructural deficits now benefit from significantly improved learning environments [8].
