**Domestic Politics and Governance**

Malta's Labour Party secured a fourth consecutive electoral victory in the 2026 general election, with Prime Minister Robert Abela taking the oath of office following the historic win [3]. Electoral Commissioner Joseph Camilleri presented the official election results to President Myriam Spiteri Debono, recording 312,129 total votes cast, of which 306,036 were valid [2][3]. Former Prime Minister Joseph Muscat described the fourth consecutive Labour victory as an important historic moment, stating that what began together in 2013 remains alive [3]. Opposition leader Alex Borg accepted the electoral verdict and congratulated Prime Minister Abela on the result [4]. The NGO Repubblika stated that Malta continues to face serious democratic challenges, including the need for stronger anti-corruption enforcement, greater transparency and accountability, more effective institutional safeguards, protection of media freedom, and full implementation of recommendations from the public inquiry into Daphne Caruana Galizia's assassination [1].

**Economy, Trade, and Labor**

An explosion occurred at the Ta' Lourdes fireworks factory in Magħtab, injuring two men who were taken to Mater Dei Hospital with minor injuries [4]. The incident had impact not only on farmers and local residents but also on the broader community [4]. The MaYA Foundation (Malta Youth in Agriculture Foundation) expressed solidarity with farmers and herders affected by the Magħtab explosion [2][3]. A number of animals died following the powerful explosions that shook the Magħtab area early in the morning [5]. The Police Officers Union expressed sincere gratitude to all police officers for their exceptional work during the long and demanding days following the incident [3].
