Religious tensions escalated in Oromia region's Arsi zone, where the Ethiopian Orthodox Church's East Arsi Diocese reported 13 Orthodox believers killed in attacks by armed groups across Aseko, Shirka, and Honqolo Wabe districts over Sunday and Monday [1]. The violence displaced over 280 congregants and resulted in the complete destruction by fire of the 101-year-old Teleta Chefa St. Gabriel Church [1]. Diocese officials indicated similar attacks occurred in Kara kebele of Aseko district, forcing residents to flee to an area called Sunte [1].

The committee had rejected the government's previous proposal last week, which came after bilateral creditor nations dismissed a January agreement between the government and bondholders' committee as incompatible with G20 framework principles [1]. The International Election Monitoring Commission declared that fundamental human rights violations did not occur during Ethiopia's election and that the process met international electoral standards [1].
