Ethiopia's $1 billion bondholders committee has indicated willingness to consider another new proposal from the Ethiopian government regarding debt restructuring, according to Reuters reporting [1]. However, certain Ethiopian bondholders have expressed intentions to pursue legal action through the courts [1]. The bondholders committee had rejected the Ethiopian government's previous proposal last week [1]. Ethiopia presented this new proposal to the bondholders committee after the bilateral creditor countries committee rejected a January agreement between the government and committee, stating it did not align with Group of 20 framework principles [1]. The Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) described yesterday's general election in Ethiopia as "peaceful," "participatory," and "inclusive" [1]. IEBC stated that no fundamental human rights violations occurred in connection with the election and that the election met international electoral standards, according to Ethiopia Insider reporting [1]. The commission's monitoring teams covered more than 180 electoral constituencies, and of 104 complaints received from various areas, 93 were resolved through information exchange with the election board [1]. Four complaints remain under process, while seven require additional information [1].

The airline indicated that flights to Kuwait will restart from June 9, according to information released today [1]. Ethiopian Orthodox Church authorities in the Eastern Arsi Diocese reported that 13 Orthodox believers were killed in what they termed "religion-based attacks" by armed groups in the Aseko, Shirka, and Honqolo Wabe districts of the Arsi Zone in Oromia Region over Sunday and Monday [1]. The diocese announced that more than 280 believers were displaced from their homes and that the 101-year-old Tseleta Chefa St. Gabriel Church was completely burned down [1]. The Head of Clergy Affairs for Aseko district, Rev. Yilma Terefe, told Wazema that armed groups conducted similar attacks in the Kara kebele of Aseko district [1]. Rev. Yilma stated that residents fled the attacks and are being displaced to a location called "Sunte" [1].
