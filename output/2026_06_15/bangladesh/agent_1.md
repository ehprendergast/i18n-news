Bangladesh's political landscape has undergone significant transitions following the departure of former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina [7]. The Awami League, which had governed since 2008, was removed from power, leading to the establishment of a new parliament that has subsequently cancelled several accountability reforms implemented after Hasina's ouster [7]. The country's political system had previously alternated between the Bangladesh Nationalist Party and Awami League from 1991 to 2008, with periods of military-backed rule interspersed throughout its post-independence history [6].

The economy has maintained steady growth averaging approximately 6.25 percent annually over the past two decades [6]. Poverty rates declined substantially from 11.8 percent in 2010 to 5.0 percent in 2022, based on the international poverty line of $2.15 per day using 2017 purchasing power parity exchange rates [6]. Bangladesh is scheduled to graduate from the United Nations' Least Developed Countries list in 2026 with assistance from international development programmes [6]. The country demonstrated resilience during the COVID-19 pandemic recovery, though economic challenges persist [6].

Regional agricultural concerns have emerged as farmers across South Asia, including Bangladesh, face fertiliser shortages and rising costs as the sowing season approaches [7]. The UN Development Programme has warned that disruptions to fuel and fertiliser supplies could negatively impact crop yields [7]. Bangladesh's agricultural sector remains central to its economy, supported by the fertile alluvial plains of the Ganges-Brahmaputra Delta [4].

Bengali language resources continue to expand digitally, with online dictionary platforms serving the world's fourth-largest language by speakers [2]. Sistech Digital has developed what it describes as Bangladesh's first online Bengali dictionary, allowing users to translate between Bengali and English without requiring additional software installation [2]. The platform features extensive vocabulary databases and user-friendly interfaces designed to serve Bengali speakers globally [2].

Cricket has provided notable achievements for Bangladesh, with the national team securing its maiden home Test victory against Pakistan through a strong bowling performance by Nahid Rana, who took five second-innings wickets [7]. A former Bangladesh captain has assumed leadership of the cricket board after the previous board was dissolved [7].

Social customs in Bangladesh reflect traditional values emphasising family structures and respect hierarchies [5]. Extended families often share dwellings due to economic necessity, though nuclear family arrangements are gaining popularity among younger generations [5]. The society maintains male-dominated characteristics with social classes playing an integral but diminishing role [5]. Marriage arrangements frequently involve intermediaries known as "ghataks," who may be family friends or relatives facilitating formal proposals between families [5].

Kabaddi serves as Bangladesh's national sport, featuring two teams of twelve players each, with seven active players per team competing in two twenty-minute halves [5]. The sport involves teams attempting to touch or capture opposing players to score points [5]. Rice constitutes the primary staple food, typically accompanied by spicy preparations and vegetables that are commonly fried, with masoor dal representing one of the most frequently consumed dishes [5].
