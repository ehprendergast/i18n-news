Nigeria occupies a strategic position in West Africa, bordered by the Republic of Benin to the west, Niger to the north, Chad to the northeast, and Cameroon to the east [1]. The country extends along 853 kilometers of Atlantic Ocean coastline in the Gulf of Guinea, covering a total area of approximately 923,769 square kilometers [1]. Nigeria's geographical coordinates place it between 4° and 14° north latitude and 3° and 15° east longitude, positioning it as a key regional power in West African affairs [2].

The country's diverse topography spans from northern savanna regions suited for cattle herding and grain cultivation to the semi-arid Sahel zone characterized by sparse rainfall and drought-resistant agriculture [1]. Central highlands, including the Jos Plateau reaching 1,295 meters in elevation, contain significant mineral resources and support tin mining operations [1]. The Guinea Savanna region serves as the backbone of Nigeria's agricultural economy, with extensive cultivation of yam, cassava, and maize [1].

Nigeria's southern regions transition into rainforest belt areas marked by heavy rainfall and dense vegetation, though extensive deforestation has occurred due to logging and agricultural conversion [1]. The Niger Delta coastal region, characterized by mangrove forests, swamps, and lagoons, forms the centre of the country's petroleum industry while facing environmental challenges including oil spills and land degradation [1]. The Niger River system, including its tributaries, drains through this delta into the Atlantic Ocean [1].

**KEY DEVELOPMENTS**
• Nigeria borders four countries and maintains 853 kilometers of Atlantic coastline
• The Jos Plateau region supports active tin mining operations at elevations up to 1,295 meters
• Guinea Savanna areas dominate agricultural production of yam, cassava, and maize
• Niger Delta region serves as the hub of petroleum industry operations
• Extensive deforestation has affected southern rainforest belt areas
• Northern Sahel zones experience sparse, unpredictable rainfall patterns
