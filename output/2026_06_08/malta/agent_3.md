Malta's political landscape operates within the framework of a parliamentary republic, with the capital Valletta serving as the administrative and commercial hub [4]. The nation is subdivided into 68 localities across its three inhabited islands of Malta, Gozo, and Comino [4]. Valletta, occupying just 0.61 square kilometers, holds the distinction of being both the southernmost capital of Europe and the smallest capital city within the European Union [4].

The Maltese economy has demonstrated notable growth trajectory, with the country registering the second-largest economic expansion in the European Union between the first quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026 [2]. This performance places Malta among the stronger economic performers within the bloc during this period.

Maltese society reflects centuries of Mediterranean cultural convergence, with family structures maintaining traditional hierarchies where men serve as heads of households while women exercise considerable domestic authority [3]. Children typically maintain close family ties, and social gatherings represent a favored activity among the population [3]. The Maltese are characterized by their hospitality and generosity toward visitors [3]. Style and quality of clothing serve as indicators of social status, with residents following European fashion trends while paying particular attention to hairstyle and personal presentation [3].

Cultural exchange initiatives have expanded Malta's international artistic connections, with a Chinese Guqin concert held in June 2026 serving to strengthen cultural ties between China and Malta [2]. The event represents part of broader cultural diplomacy efforts linking the Mediterranean nation with Asia. Malta's cuisine reflects its geographic position and historical influences, combining Italian, British, and Arabic flavors developed through centuries of cultural intersection [1]. French and Sicilian influences are particularly pronounced in local food traditions [1]. Soccer remains the most popular sport among Maltese residents [3].
