# Croatia: International Briefing

*Report date: December 19, 2024*

Limited information is available regarding Croatia's current political and governance developments from verified sources during this reporting period.

Croatian society continues to demonstrate engagement with traditional religious observances, as evidenced by Zagreb's hosting of the eighth 'Antunovski hod mladih' (Anthony March of Youth) religious event [20]. Thousands of young people participated in a 12-kilometer walk from Dubrava to the basilica of Saint Anthony of Padua in Črnomerec [20]. The religious march employed the slogan 'Ako imaš Boga, imaš sve' (If you have God, you have everything), reflecting continued spiritual engagement among Croatian youth [20].

Religious leadership maintained active involvement in youth outreach initiatives. Zagreb Archbishop Dražen Kutleša conveyed a message about participants embarking on a path of faith [20]. Fra Stjepan Brčina addressed the youth during the central Eucharistic celebration [20].

Croatian sports infrastructure continues developing through specialized training programs. Nenad Šoštarić, former coach of Croatia's women's handball national team, will lead the first summer camp for Women's Handball Club Poreč [21]. The handball camp is scheduled from June 14-19 [21].

The Istria region maintains its reputation as a significant contributor to Croatian handball excellence. Istria has produced numerous exceptional handball players including Eveline Galo, Adrijana Prosenjak, Ingrid Zulijani, Rena Lepoglavec, Nina Jukopile, and Tena Japundža [21]. These handball players wrote important pages of Croatian handball history [21].

Poreč has been synonymous with handball for many years, hosting world and European championships [21]. The handball camp will focus on individual training, modern training methods, and developing a winning mentality [21]. The camp will gather young handball players from Croatia and abroad [21].

## Key Developments

• Thousands of Croatian youth participated in Zagreb's eighth annual religious pilgrimage, demonstrating continued engagement with traditional Catholic practices
• Religious leadership emphasized themes of faith and spiritual guidance for young participants in organized cultural events
• Croatian sports infrastructure expanded with the establishment of the first professional handball summer camp in Poreč
• The Istria region's contribution to Croatian handball excellence was recognized through the involvement of accomplished former national team coaching staff
• International participation was incorporated into Croatian youth sports development programs, with the handball camp welcoming players from abroad
