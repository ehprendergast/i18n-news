Philippines: International Briefing

Report date: December 19, 2024

Ferdinand Marcos Jr. serves as president of the Philippines, having won the May 2022 election [6]. Sara Duterte holds the position of vice-president [6]. This marks the return of the Marcos family to the highest office in the archipelago nation, decades after the elder Ferdinand Marcos was ousted from power.

The Philippine peso currently fluctuates between 50-54 pesos to the US dollar [9]. This exchange rate reflects the ongoing dynamics of the Southeast Asian economy as it navigates global financial markets and regional trade relationships.

The Philippines comprises a Southeast Asian archipelago of over 7,600 islands, with a population exceeding 115 million [6][7][8][10]. This demographic scale positions the country as one of the most populous nations in the region, with significant implications for domestic governance and regional influence.

Cultural institutions across Metro Manila demonstrated public engagement through coordinated programming during International Museum Day 2026. Museums offered free admission to visitors, with participating institutions including the Yuchengco Museum, Metropolitan Museum of Manila, and National Museum [11]. These initiatives reflect efforts to broaden access to cultural heritage and educational resources.

Filipino popular music has expanded its international reach, with P-pop groups SB19, BINI, and BGYO developing fanbases beyond national borders [10]. This cultural export represents a growing soft power component of Philippine international presence.

The Filipino diaspora maintains active cultural connections through organized celebrations. In the UAE, the community staged Kalayaan 2026, described as the Middle East's largest Philippine Independence Day celebration [12]. The event featured performances by actress-singer Arci Muñoz and P-pop groups G22 and 1st.One [12].

These diaspora activities demonstrate the sustained cultural ties between overseas Filipino communities and their homeland. Such events serve both as expressions of national identity and as platforms for contemporary Filipino entertainment acts to reach international audiences.

The intersection of traditional commemorative events with contemporary popular culture reflects broader patterns in how Filipino cultural identity adapts to global contexts while maintaining core elements of national celebration.
