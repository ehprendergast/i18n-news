Matas Buzelis, who plays for the Chicago Bulls, will debut for Lithuania's national basketball team as the country prepares for FIBA World Cup qualifiers [25]. The young player averaged 16.3 points and 5.8 rebounds in the 2025-2026 NBA season [25].

Lithuania's basketball roster will feature several prominent NBA players for the upcoming international competitions. Jonas Valančiūnas and Kasparas Jakučionis have confirmed their participation in the summer qualifiers [25]. However, the team will be without one of its key players, as Domantas Sabonis will miss the summer competition due to knee meniscus rehabilitation [25].

The national team's competition schedule includes both preparatory and official matches across multiple venues. Lithuania will play a control match against Ukraine on June 28 in Šiauliai arena [25]. The competitive fixtures will see Lithuania face Great Britain on July 2 in Klaipėda arena, followed by a match against Italy on July 5 in Bologna [25].
